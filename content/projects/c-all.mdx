# C_ALL: Cognitive Assistance with LIDAR Localization



## Project Overview
C_ALL is an innovative assistive technology system designed to help visually impaired individuals navigate indoor environments safely and independently. The system combines LIDAR-based localization with cognitive assistance to provide real-time guidance, obstacle detection, and spatial awareness to users. My team and I had the amazing opportunity to present this project to the Board of Trustees at Stevens Institute of Technology, an honor reserved to a few. We also had the opportunity to present our project as finalists for the [Ansary Entrepreneurship Competetion](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7326392560489275392/).
